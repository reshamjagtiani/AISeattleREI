Methodology Notes

Levered IRR — cash only uses actual cash received: free cash flow after debt service each year, plus net sale proceeds (sale price − selling costs − loan payoff) in the exit year.
Levered IRR — after-tax adds the tax savings from depreciation each year (deduction × marginal tax rate) and subtracts an estimated depreciation-recapture tax in the exit year (accumulated depreciation × recapture rate) — this is a simplification; recapture rules vary by asset type and your CPA should confirm the actual treatment.
Unlevered IRR treats the deal as all-cash (no financing) for comparison — it isolates the return the real estate itself produces, before leverage.
Equity multiple = total cash distributed (including sale proceeds) ÷ equity invested.
